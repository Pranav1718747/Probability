01 Real-Life Problem & Significance

In modern forensic accounting, tax audit compliance, and research verification, manual review of millions of financial or experimental transactions is impossible. Fraudsters routinely fabricate numbers to cover shortfalls or pad expenses.

Core Psychological Conflict: Natural growth processes follow logarithmic scales, whereas humans inventing numbers instinctively distribute digits uniformly (~11.1% each) and avoid repeating digit 1.

Objective: Leverage an undergraduate statistical hypothesis test (χ² goodness-of-fit) to establish an automated, non-invasive triage tool for detecting fabricated numerical records.

02 Concepts & Hypothesis Testing

Benford's First-Digit Law: In naturally occurring unconstrained data spanning multiple orders of magnitude, the probability of leading digit d ∈ {1, …, 9} is:

P(d) = log10(1 + 1/d)

Digit 1: 30.1% | Digit 2: 17.6% | Digit 3: 12.5% … Digit 9: 4.6%

Statistical Hypotheses (Module 6):

  • H0: First-digit distribution follows Benford's Law.
  • H1: First-digit distribution departs from Benford's Law.

Pearson's Chi-Square Test Statistic:

χ² = ∑i=19 (Oi - Ei)² / Ei,    Ei = n · P(i)

Degrees of Freedom: df = k - 1 - m = 9 - 1 - 0 = 8 (no parameters estimated from sample).
Critical Value (α = 0.05, df = 8): χ²crit = 15.507.

Cochran's Condition: Ei ≥ 5 for all bins &implies; n ≥ 110. World Bank n = 217 yields E9 = 9.93 > 5.0 (Passed).

06 The River Lengths Paradox

The historical North American rivers dataset (n = 141) failed Benford's test (χ² = 44.18, p < 0.001). Why?

Selection Truncation Effect: The USGS World Almanac recorded only "major" rivers (>135 miles). Omitting small streams broke scale invariance, clustering values between 200–400 miles (digit 3 count: 36 vs exp 17.6).

Vital Lesson: A rejection is NOT proof of fraud! Bounded or truncated authentic datasets naturally depart from Benford.

07 Conclusions & Audit Limits
  • Clear Contrast: Genuine data conformed (χ² = 7.608, p = 0.473), whereas uniform fabrication failed overwhelmingly (χ² = 79.982, p = 4.93×10-14).
  • Effect Size is Mandatory: With large samples, χ² rejects tiny deviations. Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) provides scale-free practical significance.
  • Audit Ethics: Rejection flags anomalies for manual review; failure to reject does not guarantee innocence.
